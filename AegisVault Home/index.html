CRYPTOGRAPHIC RESILIENCE

Built for What Matters Most.

Enterprise-grade cryptographic security engineered with zero compromises. Your secrets never exist in plain text outside your authorized devices.

Client-Side Encryption

All payload encryption occurs locally in your browser using AES-256-GCM before transmission. Data leaves your possession as unreadable ciphertext.

AES-256-GCM

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

AegisVault operates on mathematical zero knowledge. We do not possess master keys, decryption passwords, or backdoor credentials under any circumstance.

Zero-Knowledge

Threshold Secret Sharing

Key recovery leverages Shamir's Secret Sharing (k-of-n). Master keys are split into multiple cryptographic shards that require trustee consensus to reconstruct.

Shamir's SSS

Multi-Factor Authentication

FIDO2 WebAuthn passkeys, hardware YubiKeys, and time-based one-time passwords (TOTP) safeguard vault modifications against unauthorized access.

FIDO2 / WebAuthn

Trustee Verification

Designated legacy trustees undergo rigorous identity verification protocols and multi-signature confirmations before any custody release countdown completes.

Multi-Sig Verified

Immutable Audit Logs

Every heartbeat check-in, trustee invitation, and security parameter adjustment is permanently recorded to a verifiable cryptographic audit ledger.

Append-Only Ledger
