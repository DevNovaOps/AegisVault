Our Origin Story

Founded in 2024 by veteran cryptographers and security engineers, AegisVault was born from a painful reality: each year, billions in digital assets, family archives, and intellectual property vanish forever because estate planning failed to keep pace with the digital age.

We engineered an autonomous custody protocol combining time-lock smart contracts, threshold cryptography, and multi-factor heartbeat surveillance — giving individuals and institutions unbreakable sovereignty over their lifetime achievements.
