01. Zero-Knowledge Commitment

AegisVault ("Protocol", "we", "us") operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge paradigm. All digital assets, files, credentials, and cryptographic shares stored within your vaults are encrypted client-side using authenticated AES-256-GCM. We possess neither the mathematical capability nor the architectural keys to decrypt, inspect, or disclose your plaintext vault contents.

02. Telemetry & Account Data

To maintain the autonomous heartbeat protocol and deliver notifications, we process minimal operational metadata:

  • Account email addresses and optional phone numbers for heartbeat verification pings.
  • Public cryptographic identity keys (WebAuthn credentials and public shard handles).
  • System logs recording check-in timestamps, threshold fulfillment events, and node health telemetry.

03. Data Retention & Erasure

You retain absolute mathematical sovereignty over your vault records. If you execute a "Vault Purge" from the Owner Command Center, ciphertext payloads, cryptographic shares, and associated metadata are immediately overwritten and permanently erased using cryptographic multi-pass zeroization standards.

04. Third-Party Disclosure

Because we do not store plaintext encryption keys, subpoena requests or government inquiries cannot compel plaintext revelation of your stored assets. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal telemetry under any circumstances.

REVISION: V4.2 ENTERPRISE AUTONOMOUS CUSTODY AGREEMENT

01. Non-Custodial Architecture

AegisVault is a non-custodial cryptographic management protocol. You maintain primary responsibility for the preservation of your account authentication credentials, emergency recovery codes, and trustee appointments. AegisVault does not act as a traditional fiduciary or financial depository.

02. Heartbeat & Release Conditions

By setting heartbeat intervals, you explicitly authorize the AegisVault automated release engine to dispatch threshold claims to your designated trustees upon the expiration of your configured check-in grace period. You agree to maintain active communication channels to prevent unintentional trigger execution.

03. Trustee Fiduciary Conduct

Designated trustees must independently satisfy any identity verification standards established by the vault owner. AegisVault enforces mathematical threshold compliance ($M\text{-of-}N$) but is not liable for interpersonal disputes among appointed trustees.

04. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, AegisVault shall not be liable for losses resulting from lost user credentials, forgotten passwords, compromised hardware keys, or failure of trustees to claim and reconstruct shards within specified timeframes.

Technical Protocol Specification

Complete mathematical and architectural reference for AegisVault custody workflows.

1. Client-Side Encryption (AES-256-GCM)

Every payload is encrypted using a unique 256-bit symmetric data key generated via cryptographically secure pseudo-random numbers (CSPRNG). The master key is derived with Argon2id using salt length $\ge 128$ bits.

// Cryptographic Initialization Vector (IV) & Cipher Spec: Cipher: AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) IV Length: 96 bits (12 bytes) CSPRNG unique per encryption Auth Tag: 128 bits authenticated integrity verification Key Derivation: Argon2id (m=64MB, t=4, p=4)

2. Heartbeat Ping Endpoint

Owners can automate heartbeats via CLI scripts, hardware tokens, or curl triggers with JWT authentication:

POST /api/v1/owner/heartbeat/ping/ HTTP/1.1 Host: aegisvault.io Authorization: Bearer <aegis_access_token> Content-Type: application/json { "client_timestamp": "2026-10-21T17:00:00Z", "client_signature": "0x4a9b...ed25519" } // Response: HTTP 200 OK { "status": "HEARTBEAT_RECORDED", "next_check_in_due": "2026-11-20T17:00:00Z", "grace_period_days": 14 }

3. Shamir M-of-N Reconstruction

Secret sharing polynomials are evaluated over the Galois Field $GF(2^8)$. Given polynomial $f(x) = a_0 + a_1 x + \dots + a_{M-1} x^{M-1}$ where $a_0$ is the Master Secret, Lagrange interpolation reconstructs $f(0)$ with any $M$ coordinates $(x_i, y_i)$.

f(0) = \sum_{j=1}^{M} y_j \prod_{k \neq j} \frac{x_k}{x_k - x_j} \pmod{P(x)} Threshold Condition: |Shares_submitted| >= M
