To appoint a trustee, go to Assigned Vaults, open the vault configuration, and select “Appoint Trustee”. Provide their legal name and verified email. They will receive an appointment invitation with an encrypted onboarding link to verify their identity.
Once a vault release is initiated, appointed trustees have a grace period window (default 48 to 72 hours) to submit cryptographic fragments. As soon as the designated Shamir quorum threshold (e.g., 2 of 3) is submitted, institutional validation completes within 15 minutes.
Submitted shares are cryptographically committed to the consensus queue. If you made an error or suspect security compromise, you must initiate an emergency freeze in Profile & Security or alert support immediately to nullify the pending ticket.
If an appointed trustee declines an invitation or release petition, the vault owner is immediately notified. If a secondary or alternate trustee is configured in the vault policy, the prompt automatically falls back to them to maintain quorum continuity.
