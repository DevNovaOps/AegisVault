Submission Guidelines

Keep Your Share Secure Never share your original key fragment or recovery passphrase via email, chat, or unencrypted channels.
Verify Vault Identity Ensure the vault name and owner name match your designated appointment before authorizing submission.
Client-Side Cryptography Your browser computes local cryptographic hashes and encrypts your share with ephemeral keys.
Quorum Threshold Required Release requires 2 of 3 trustee shares to reconstruct the master vault key. No single trustee can unlock a vault.
Grace Period Notice The vault owner is notified immediately upon initial share submission with a 14-day cancellation window.

Need Help?

A cryptographic share is an encrypted piece of the master vault key created using Shamir's Secret Sharing scheme (M-of-N threshold). It contains zero readable user data by itself.
Your share was provided during trustee appointment, either downloaded as a .share or .key file, or stored in your designated hardware security module.
Once submitted during an active release window, a share enters cryptographic consensus. During the grace period, the vault owner can cancel the release process.
It is a mathematical algorithm that divides a secret into unique parts. A predetermined minimum number of shares (e.g. 2 of 3) must be combined to reconstruct the secret.
