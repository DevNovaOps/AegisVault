Immutable Activity Ledger

Every interaction within AegisVault — including key share submissions, vault reviews, trustee verifications, and logins — is recorded in an immutable, append-only cryptographic audit ledger.

Cryptographic Timestamping: Each record contains a microsecond NTP timestamp validated against decentralized consensus time servers.
Device & IP Attribution: Strict originating network signatures prevent fraudulent spoofing and preserve non-repudiation for legal executors.
