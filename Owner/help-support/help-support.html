Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions.

AegisVault is an enterprise-grade digital legacy and dead-man's-switch platform. It uses zero-knowledge client-side encryption (AES-256-GCM) to safeguard your digital assets, documents, and credentials, releasing them to your designated trustees only when verified triggers or inactivity conditions are fulfilled.

Navigate to the "My Vaults" tab on the left navigation. Click the "+ Create Vault" button, choose a category icon, specify a name, upload your encrypted files, and set your access control parameters. You can edit, lock, or delete vaults at any time.

Open the "Trustees" page and click "+ Add Trustee". Enter their full name, email address, relationship, and assign the vaults they should receive. An encrypted 72-hour invitation link is dispatched for their biometric or passkey verification.

You can recover account access using your 24-word Emergency Master Recovery Key provided during registration. Without this recovery key or your master password, zero-knowledge encrypted vaults cannot be decrypted by anyone.
